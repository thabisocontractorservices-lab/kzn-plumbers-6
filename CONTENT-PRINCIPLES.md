# KZN Plumbers Content and Data Standard

## The rule

Publish fewer pages with more local utility. A page must help a homeowner choose, verify, contact, or prepare for a plumbing job in KwaZulu-Natal.

## Evidence hierarchy

Use information in this order:

1. Current first-party marketplace data with sample size and date
2. Official national, provincial, municipal, PIRB, IOPSA, or standards guidance
3. A named qualified reviewer
4. A clearly attributed, reputable public source
5. A business-supplied claim labelled as supplied and not independently confirmed

Do not turn an unsupported statement into a fact because it appears in several directories.

## Prohibited content

- Town-name swaps using the same article template
- Invented rates, availability, response times, credentials, reviews, experience, guarantees, insurance, or service areas
- Claiming every imported record is verified
- Synthetic testimonials
- Generic “top plumber” rankings without a published method
- Pages created only to redirect users to the homepage
- AI-written biography padding from a business name and area
- Unsourced legal or compliance claims
- Copied municipal notices without a source, publication date, and update status

## Minimum standard for an indexable regional or service page

A page should contain:

- Live relevant provider inventory near the top
- A clear explanation of the verification labels
- Unique local or service-specific decision support
- A source and review date for external facts
- Direct, measurable contact actions
- Honest empty-state behaviour when no providers match
- Links to the relevant region, service, trust method, and provider profiles
- A canonical URL and deliberate sitemap status

If a page cannot meet that standard, merge it, noindex it, redirect it to a reviewed replacement, or remove it.

## AI use

AI may assist with classification, duplicate detection, editing, and drafting from verified facts. It must not invent missing business data or generate many pages from a location list.

Every AI-assisted public draft requires:

- Provenance for factual claims
- A check against the actual provider inventory
- A uniqueness review against related pages
- A human publish decision
- A review or expiry date

## Voice

- Helpful, specific, calm, and local
- South African English spelling
- Short sentences where urgency matters
- No fear-based sales language
- No empty claims such as “best”, “trusted”, “leading”, or “verified” without evidence
- Explain limitations directly

## Original KZN resources

A cost index, outage desk, response benchmark, or annual verification report may be published only when the underlying method is visible. Show:

- Data period
- Sample size
- Inclusion and exclusion rules
- Median or distribution rather than a misleading single average
- Geographic coverage
- Last update date
- Known limitations

Do not publish a cost benchmark from a handful of quotes or copy national prices into a KZN-branded page.
